## About These Notes

These notes collect personal learning and project experience across agent applications, LLM fundamentals, machine learning, software engineering, and multimodal evaluation. They are intended for both structured study and quick reference.

The notes are updated over time and may contain gaps or outdated information. Suggestions, corrections, and clearer explanations are welcome.

## Start Here

If you are new to AI agents, this sequence introduces the core ideas:

1. [Agent Fundamentals and Beyond](/notes/agent): Learn the key concepts, common architectures, and design patterns behind agents.
2. [Function Calling, MCP, and Skills](/notes/tools): Understand how models use tools and how these approaches organize capabilities.
3. [RAG](/notes/rag): Learn how retrieval can provide models with external knowledge.

You can also use the search box in the navigation bar to find a topic or term.
